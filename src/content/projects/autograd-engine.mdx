## What it does

A `Value` object wraps a number and remembers how it was made. Calling `backward()` sorts the graph topologically and applies the chain rule from the output back to every input.

On top of it sits a small neural network module with neurons, layers and a multi-layer perceptron. It follows the design of micrograd. A sanity-check script runs both parts.
