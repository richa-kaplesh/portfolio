## What it does

Upload a PDF, ask a question, and get an answer with the source chunks shown, so you can verify it.

## How it works

LangChain splits the document into 500-character chunks and embeds them with all-MiniLM-L6-v2. FAISS finds the three closest chunks to the question. Llama 3.3 70B on Groq answers from those chunks only.

Everything tunable, including the model name, lives in one config file. When Groq retired a model, the fix was one line.

The [README](https://github.com/richa-kaplesh/document-qa#readme) goes through the chain step by step.
