## What it does

Paste a news headline and the model says whether it looks real or fake, with a confidence score. A dashboard shows which sources publish the most fake news.

## How it was built

The dataset is FakeNewsNet, 76% real and 24% fake. The baseline reached 83% accuracy but found only 46% of the fake articles, so accuracy alone was misleading.

Adding the source domain raised fake recall to 74%. Standard text cleaning made results worse, because punctuation in headlines carries signal. Giving the model each source's share of fake articles raised fake recall to 79%.

Six algorithms were then compared on a train, validation and test split. Gradient boosting had the best validation accuracy, 89.83%. Random forest came close but had memorised its training set.

The [README](https://github.com/richa-kaplesh/Fake-News-Detector#readme) records every experiment, including the ones that failed.
