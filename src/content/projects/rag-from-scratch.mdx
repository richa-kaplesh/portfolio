## What it does

Upload a PDF and ask questions about it. Each stage of the pipeline is its own small Python file, so every step can be read and changed.

## Choices worth noting

Text is extracted page by page, so an answer can point back to the page it came from. Chunks are 500 characters with 50 of overlap, which keeps a sentence at a boundary whole in at least one chunk.

Vector search is cosine similarity written in NumPy. Embeddings come from all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and answers from Llama 3.1 8B on Groq.

The [README](https://github.com/richa-kaplesh/rag-from-scratch#readme) explains the reason behind each file.
